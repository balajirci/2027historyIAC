Red Set Regional Simulator - History Bee

IAC regional format, Red set bank of 544 tossups. Three preliminary rounds of 30, a top-half qualification cut, then a 30-question final from the hardest pool. Geography simulator: red-geography-simulator.html  ·  Science simulator: red-science-simulator.html
How it runs
Questions read word by word, hardest clues first, exactly like a moderator - and the other 11 players in the room are live. Any rival can buzz mid-question: if they are right, the question is gone and you score 0 on it; if they are wrong, they take -5, are locked out, and the reading continues for everyone else. Buzz the moment you know it: first third 15 points, middle third 12, late 10. Your own wrong buzz is -5, and the field then gets a rebound chance on the rest of the question. If nobody converts and the question finishes, you get a short final window to buzz for 10. Type your answer or mark "answered aloud" before the answer is revealed. The rival field is a practice model; the real cut on Dec 5-6 is the top half of your actual room.
Listen only is the tournament test: the question is spoken once, and the text appears only after you buzz or the answer is revealed. Buzzing stops the voice. Best with a headset.
